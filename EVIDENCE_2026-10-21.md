## The growth equation (Christopher's derivation)

The expansion rule for the bits (fan-out) has been established
since 2026-10-18 and verified exhaustively. What was missing was
an equation for the expansion itself — the layer widths. On
2026-10-21 Christopher derived it from the established numbers
(layers 0–3: widths 4, 8, 128, 32768):

    W(n) = 2^(2^(n+1) − 1)          n = layer, starting at 1

Equivalent forms, all verified to agree:

    implied bits = combinations / 2,  combinations = 2^g,
    g = 2^(n+1) generating bits (4, 8, 16, 32, 64, …)
    W(n+1) = 2 · W(n)²

Widths: 8, 128, 32768, then predictions 2^31 (layer 4),
2^63 (layer 5), 2^127 (layer 6). Exponents are 3, 7, 15, 31,
63, 127 — each 2^k − 1. Fan factors under the law:
2, 16, 256, 65536, 2^32 (the first three are the established
canon steps). The growth of implied positions is now specified
at every layer.

His objective, in his words (2026-10-21): "The objective isn't
to multiply controllers. It's to determine what one controller
can imply." The architecture is single-controller: expansion
multiplies implied positions, never controllers.

## Corrections of record (2026-10-21)

Three earlier framings were examined and withdrawn today:

1. **"The original equation."** Earlier notes spoke of an
   original equation of Christopher's as lost/unrecovered.
   Record check: no evidence he ever had one. What existed was
   the idea and, from 2026-10-16, his explanation of it. The
   missing piece was an equation for the (already known)
   expansion — supplied by his derivation above.
2. **"Alternate layers."** Christopher's 2026-10-18 remark that
   the layers "alternate" (then unexplained) is resolved by him:
   it was the expansion itself, imagined as alternating — each
   layer's output becoming the next layer's input. Closed.
3. **"Composition between spaces."** Closed by premise: two
   independent controllers are two separate spaces; merging them
   would change the premise. The open direction is what can be
   expressed *within* one space (operations, relationships,
   bindings). Any future interaction between spaces is to be
   defined through UCE rules, not an invented merging mechanism.

## Tests run today (all in this repository)

**Expansion rule test** (`expansion_rule_test.py`, log included):
fan-out + the growth equation. Layers 1–3 exhaustive vs the
materialized chain: 526,464 checks, 0 failures. Layers 4–5
on demand (block/quarter boundaries + seeded random positions,
all 16 controllers): 1,601,920 checks, step-by-step provenance
== closed form, 0 failures. Ones law — ones(v, n) =
popcount(v) × W(n)/4 — verified by block aggregation, layers
0–5. Scope: above layer 3 there is no independent canon; the
test proves the extension is coherent and every invariant from
established layers continues under the equation.

**Producer test** (`implied_space_producer.py`, log included):
a `ProducedSpace` class knowing only the 4 controller bits and
the expansion rule (widths from the equation, fan factors as
width ratios, nothing hardcoded) produces the space. Matches
the chain construction exhaustively at layers 1–3 (526,464
checks) and an independent windowed-recursive production at
layers 4–5 (400 windows, 12,800 bits), 0 failures throughout.

**Space battery** (`implication_space_test.py`, log included):
the produced space against every property this project has
claimed — determinism (2,000 queries × 4 shuffled passes, pass);
direct == recursive production at layers 4–5 (pass);
no materialization (0 KB peak-memory growth over 20,000 deep
queries); per-query cost 482 ns (layer 1) to 2,859 ns (layer 5)
— grows with layer count, never with width; all 16 spaces
pairwise distinct at layers 1–5; random Boolean functions of
implied bits across layers answered from the controller alone
(3,000 trials, pass); controller recovery from corrupted
layer-3 samples (2,000 bits, majority vote per quarter):
200/200 exact at 5%, 20%, 35% corruption, 191/200 at 45%
(statistical, near the 50% theoretical wall — consistent with
the 2026-10-18 addressing result).

**Closed form for bit values** (`equation_hunt.py`, log
included): collapsing the fan-out chain gives B(v, W, j) = the
q-th controller bit, q = ⌊4j/W⌋ — a position's bit is the
controller bit chosen at the first step of its path. Verified
against the chain construction at all established widths
(526,464 checks, 0 mismatches), his four examples exact.

**UCE pass** (`uce_bits_and_size.py`, log included): the
Universal Concept Engine run over two variables — "infinite
bits" and "the size of the model" — with negative controls.
Findings: "infinite" binds to *access* (unbounded answerability
from a finite origin), never to storage or freedom; in "model
size," the count role survived no comparison, while capability
tracked the count of independent learned values in every
pairing. (The engine compares structures; the interpretations
are recorded as interpretations, in the log.)

**Numbers sheet** (`numbers_to_layer3.py`, log included):
every established number up to layer 3 — widths, exponents,
fan factors, copies per real bit, ones counts, distances,
pattern tables — computed from the canon. The working sheet
from which the growth equation was derived.
